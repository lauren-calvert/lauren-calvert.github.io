Write down the essentials, plan a gradual handoff when possible, and leave room for questions. A simple daily rhythm, emergency contacts, favorite activities, and a check-in at the end of the week can make the transition feel far more natural.

## Prepare the practical details

Share emergency contacts, allergy information, household access instructions, preferred pediatric care information, and the routines your children already know. Keep everything in one easy-to-find place.

## Make room for relationships

Children and caregivers need time to learn one another. When possible, begin with a shared transition period and avoid over-scheduling the first few days.

## Check in intentionally

Set aside time at the end of the first day and first week. Ask what felt smooth, what was unclear, and what each person needs for the coming week.
