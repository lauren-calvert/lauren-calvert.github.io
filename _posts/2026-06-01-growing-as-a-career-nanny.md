Keep certifications current, document new skills, ask for specific feedback, and treat each role as a chance to deepen your knowledge of child development.

## Invest in your expertise

CPR and First Aid are the foundation. Additional training in newborn care, child development, special needs support, nutrition, or household management can help you qualify for more specialized roles.

## Keep a professional record

Maintain an updated résumé, written references, certification dates, and a clear list of responsibilities from past positions. This makes it easier to communicate the full value of your experience.

## Protect clear boundaries

Professionalism includes a written work agreement, defined hours, clear duties, and open communication about schedule changes. Strong boundaries support both the caregiver and the family.
