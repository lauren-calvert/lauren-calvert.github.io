A strong working relationship begins with specificity. Talk through a normal day, what “light household support” means in your home, how updates should be shared, and how both sides prefer to give feedback.

## Start with the daily rhythm

Walk through arrival, meals, naps, school transportation, activities, and the end-of-day handoff. The clearer the ordinary day feels, the easier it is to recognize whether a candidate’s experience and working style align with your family.

## Define responsibilities together

Child-related tasks can mean different things to different families. Discuss meal preparation, laundry, organization, driving, and household support explicitly. Clear expectations are kinder than assumptions.

## Plan for communication and change

Agree on how often you will check in, where schedule changes should be shared, and how both sides can raise concerns early. A short weekly conversation during the first month can prevent small questions from becoming larger misunderstandings.
