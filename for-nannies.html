Candidate requirements

What we look for

  • A passion for safe, creative, enriching care
  • Professional teaching, nanny, or childcare experience
  • Strong childcare and character references
  • Clean background check and suitable driving record
  • Current CPR and First Aid certification
  • Non-smoking status
  • Ability to commit to a family’s agreed schedule
  • Valid driver’s license or vehicle when a role requires it
