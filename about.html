Our story

Care built on listening.

A local agency created to help families find thoughtful, dependable support without navigating the search alone.

Meet Paige Davis

From postpartum support to lasting childcare.

Paige is a Huntsville native who began her specialized career in doula work while living in Seattle. After training at Bastyr University’s Penny Simkin Center, she worked with a boutique nanny agency serving busy professional families.

Back in Huntsville, Paige founded Imagine Birth Doula. Families consistently shared the same need: they valued hands-on postpartum support and wanted a seamless way to continue high-quality, one-on-one care when they returned to work.

Imagine Care Nannies grew from that need—a calm, guided way for families to find qualified care and for professional nannies to find roles where they can thrive.

The right placement should feel considered, communicative, and built to last.
What we offer

A full-service boutique search

Focused recruiting, careful screening, and hands-on guidance from the first conversation through placement.

Specialized recruitment

Nannies, newborn care specialists, household managers, and caregiver helpers selected for your specific role.

Thoughtful screening

Interviews, references, background checks, and driving record reports for your selected candidate.

One-on-one guidance

Clear communication, interview coordination, local resources, and support through the offer and onboarding process.

Focused attention

We partner with a limited number of families at a time so your search receives the care it deserves.

Training + resources

Professional development opportunities for nannies and helpful referrals for families within our community.

Replacement support

If a placement changes, we guide next steps and a new search according to the terms of your agreement.

Our standard

The right fit—not the fastest fit.

We look beyond a résumé to understand communication style, values, experience, expectations, and real-life logistics. That deeper work makes room for placements that feel steady from the beginning.

  • Carefully interviewed candidates
  • Childcare and character references
  • Background and driving review
  • Offer and onboarding guidance
  • Ongoing communication support
