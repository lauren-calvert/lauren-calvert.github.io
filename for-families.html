For families

Support shaped around your home.

A thoughtful nanny search for families who value reliable care, clear communication, and long-term compatibility.

Why families choose a nanny

Consistent care. More breathing room.

The right nanny offers more than coverage—they become a trusted partner in your family’s routines, growth, and everyday life.

Care in your home

Children stay in a familiar environment with routines and rhythms designed around your household.

Individual attention

One-on-one care supports each child’s interests, milestones, learning style, and personality.

Reliable flexibility

A schedule built for your real workday can reduce handoffs, commutes, and last-minute scrambling.

Stronger continuity

Children benefit from a dependable caregiver who knows their needs, preferences, and development.

Household support

Depending on the role, nannies or household managers can help with child-related organization and routines.

A relationship that grows

Intentional matching creates the foundation for trust between parents, children, and caregivers.

Placement process

Full-service guidance, start to finish.

Every search begins with clarity. We learn what makes your home work, build the role, identify strong candidates, and stay close through the final decision.

Request a family consultation
Step 1

Consult + clarity

A brief consultation helps us understand your family, schedule, priorities, and ideal kind of support.

Step 2

Job description + search strategy

We create a clear role and recruiting plan designed to attract candidates aligned with your expectations and household style.

Step 3

Candidates + screening

You receive a carefully selected shortlist. We conduct interviews, reference checks, and background screening before final placement.

Step 4

Interviews + offer support

We coordinate interviews, guide selection, and support the offer and onboarding process.

Included in your placement

Everything needed for a confident hire.

  • Initial needs and priorities consultation
  • Clear, targeted job description
  • Thoughtful candidate sourcing and screening
  • Shortlist of qualified candidates
  • Interview coordination and communication
  • Reference and background screening
  • Offer and hiring guidance

Exact screening steps vary by role and family preferences.
